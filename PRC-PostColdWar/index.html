Compiler Desk

Build The Book From Decision Trails

Presidential Level

Start with Bush and Clinton library records for leader meetings, NSC paper flow, daily diaries, memcons, and trade-policy binders.

Policy Linkage

Treat MFN, human rights, nonproliferation, Taiwan, and WTO as linked decisions, then track when officials separated or recombined them.

Public Case

Pair speeches, executive orders, public papers, and congressional action with the internal memoranda that framed the public argument.

Boundary Control

Keep Hong Kong, Tibet, Korea, Japan, South Asia, and arms control in view while separating evidence that belongs in adjacent regional or functional volumes.

Gate 03

Research Risk

Known Gaps And Controls

Classified Cables

Bush Tiananmen cables are described in finding aids but many remain closed. Use released public mail and staff files as surrounding evidence while tracking MDR or FOIA options.

USTR Records

Clinton Library WTO collections explicitly note limits on USTR custody. Treat USTR, Commerce, NEC, NSC, and congressional files as separate record trails.

Chinese Sources

PRC public statements and white papers are essential counterpart sources, but internal party-state archives are unevenly accessible and need separate handling.

Post-2001 Handoff

WTO accession, the EP-3 incident, and the post-9/11 recalibration belong in a follow-on dossier unless the chapter needs them as consequences.
