## Regression Expectations

- A source register or release packet must not yield a final-looking `Document [TBD]` annotation sheet unless the compiler selected the register/list itself.
- A whole-volume PDF, chapter export, table of contents, document list, or many-document packet must remain full_volume_or_chapter_packet context unless the selected document and page range are supplied: preserve volume/chapter identity, document list, page-numbering basis, front/back matter, ebook/export status, publication/update date, and candidate boundaries without drafting one annotation sheet for the whole upload or using volume-level dates/order as document date, source provenance, declassification status, document number, or manuscript placement.
- A volume Sources section or finding-aid PDF must remain source_register_or_finding_aid context: preserve source-family, repository, lot-file, published-source, and volume-context evidence, but do not convert it into a manuscript document or a document-specific Source note without selected-document evidence.
- An incremental-volume placeholder page must remain incremental_chapter_placeholder context: preserve placeholder wording, chapter-clearance status, partial Sources-section scope, official volume context, and ebook/publication date evidence, but do not draft a manuscript annotation sheet or convert it into withheld-in-full, omitted-body, OCR-failure, or source-register-only handling without the actual selected document PDF and compiler/source authority.
- A legacy digitized-volume or microfiche-preview PDF must remain legacy_digitized_or_microfiche_preview context unless one selected document and its boundaries are visible or supplied: preserve original print date, quarterly release or digitization date, preview/full-text status, front-matter booklet status, PDFs of documents bundle status, microfiche supplement status, scan/OCR quality, page labels, and fiche/frame identifiers without using digitization date as the document date, source provenance, declassification evidence, or proof of full-text conversion.
- A history.state.gov web/print export PDF must remain history_state_web_or_print_export context unless a more specific selected-document archetype is visible: preserve official URL, volume/document id, HTML anchor, print/export date, browser header/footer, page title, and static EPUB/TEI basis without using URL/header/footer/print date as source provenance, document date, classification, declassification status, document number, or manuscript placement. A shell-only export must return web_print_export_triage / not_annotation_sheet_web_export_shell_only rather than a final-looking annotation sheet.
- A fax sheet, routing slip, S/S cover sheet, or transmission cover page must remain fax_or_transmission_cover_sheet / transmission_or_routing_cover_sheet wrapper evidence unless selected by compiler instruction: preserve sender/recipient, date/time sent or received, page count, delivery instruction, routing chain, presented-to/read-by notation, S/S/control number, attached-document reference, and wrapper-payload relationship without using wrapper data as selected document date, source provenance, classification, approval, read status, or manuscript placement. A wrapper-only upload must return cover_sheet_triage / not_annotation_sheet_cover_sheet_only rather than a final-looking annotation sheet.
- A tabbed action memo must preserve the cover memorandum and attached tabs as separate candidate units.
- A freestanding policy paper with no visible author must not be recast as `Paper Prepared by...` or `Memorandum From...` without target-volume authority.
- A public-source PDF must preserve publication/event/page metadata and must not be forced into archival source-note form.
- A selected public speech/media PDF must preserve the public publication basis, event metadata, archival-copy/transmission lane, diary or schedule support, bracket/omission notes, and boundary evidence separately without replacing the selected public text with a reprint telegram or generic archive path.
- A news-conference or media-availability PDF must preserve event type, venue, event date/time, broadcast/live status, questioner identity, quoted question and answer, public transcript citation, complete-text citation, editorial-note status when applicable, and document boundaries without recasting the item as a memcon, interview, ordinary public speech, or archival source-note record.
- A press release, press guidance, or background-briefing PDF must preserve release/briefing type, issuing office or convening office, release or event date/time, venue/briefing room, speaker or attribution label, ground rules, embargo/released-text status, public transcript/version basis, carrier telegram or archival-copy lane, editorial-note status when applicable, and document boundaries without recasting the item as a memcon, interview, news conference, ordinary public speech, public-affairs strategy memorandum, or proof of classification.
- A summit public statement or joint-communique PDF must preserve the carrier telegram or public-source copy, summit/event context, public-release or briefing-use instruction, issuing leaders/parties, Begin/End statement boundaries, public/private version, treaty-or-negotiation relationship, and document boundaries without recasting the item as an ordinary telegram, a signed treaty/instrument, a treaty transmittal, or generic public-source citation.
