## Regression Expectations

- A source register or release packet must not yield a final-looking `Document [TBD]` annotation sheet unless the compiler selected the register/list itself.
- A tabbed action memo must preserve the cover memorandum and attached tabs as separate candidate units.
- A freestanding policy paper with no visible author must not be recast as `Paper Prepared by...` or `Memorandum From...` without target-volume authority.
- A public-source PDF must preserve publication/event/page metadata and must not be forced into archival source-note form.
- A selected public speech/media PDF must preserve the public publication basis, event metadata, archival-copy/transmission lane, diary or schedule support, bracket/omission notes, and boundary evidence separately without replacing the selected public text with a reprint telegram or generic archive path.
- A news-conference or media-availability PDF must preserve event type, venue, event date/time, broadcast/live status, questioner identity, quoted question and answer, public transcript citation, complete-text citation, editorial-note status when applicable, and document boundaries without recasting the item as a memcon, interview, ordinary public speech, or archival source-note record.
- A summit public statement or joint-communique PDF must preserve the carrier telegram or public-source copy, summit/event context, public-release or briefing-use instruction, issuing leaders/parties, Begin/End statement boundaries, public/private version, treaty-or-negotiation relationship, and document boundaries without recasting the item as an ordinary telegram, a signed treaty/instrument, a treaty transmittal, or generic public-source citation.
- A public-affairs strategy or outreach-plan PDF must preserve internal strategy status, author/recipient/subject, source path, classification, action/information routing, public-opinion evidence, intended audience, proposed message or public line, and speech/statement/event relationship without recasting it as public remarks, a public-source item, a final policy decision, or ordinary talking points.
- A congressional/legal public-source PDF must preserve testimony text, committee/body metadata, hearing publication citation, public-law/statutory citation, budget authority, authorization/appropriation stage, Presidential Determination, Federal Register status, and archival-copy lane separately, and must not infer enactment, approval, appropriation, advice-and-consent, or publication from a request or discussion.
- A Presidential Determination or Federal Register notice PDF must preserve determination number/date, statutory authority or trigger, finding/waiver/certification language, congressional reporting direction, Federal Register publication directive, actual Federal Register citation only when visible, attached statement of reasons or justification, archival source path, and document boundaries without treating a publication directive as proof of actual publication, congressional receipt, legal effectiveness, or fund release.
- An airgram/despatch PDF must preserve airgram/despatch number, origin post, destination, subject/ref block, referenced telegrams, source path, drafting/clearance/approval, signature/initialed-for evidence, and document boundaries without inventing ordinary cable DTG, TAGS, precedence, channel, or full telegram header metadata.
- A prepared research-report or policy-paper PDF must preserve report/control number, preparing office, title/subtitle, summary, section headings, classification or portion markings, drafting/approval evidence, reader marginalia, source path, and boundaries without recasting the selected item as a briefing memo, telegram, formal intelligence estimate, ordinary memorandum, or editorial note.
- A draft/working-copy PDF must preserve draft status, version label, coordination status, covering-memorandum evidence, final-version references, version-comparison language, agency dissent or alternative-view notes, omitted sections, and boundaries without treating the selected draft as final, coordinated, approved, sent, tabled, or published.
- A non-paper/informal-paper PDF must preserve informal selected-paper status, no-classification marking, handwritten presentation/distribution notes, diary/schedule support, readout evidence, and boundaries without inventing formal sender/recipient/signature metadata or recasting the paper as the meeting record where it was discussed.
- A Situation Room/watch-center PDF must preserve watch-product type, checklist/report title, as-of/event time, source path, classification, source telegram/report references, PSN/product identifiers, not-found identifier notes, notification/source labels, marginalia, and boundaries without recasting the product as the telegram or wire report it summarizes.
- A law-enforcement/counterterrorism case PDF must preserve incident/case identity, legal charges, warrant/red-notice/provisional-arrest/extradition status, FBI/DEA/DOJ/OIA/S/CT/Interpol lanes, counterterrorism source family, scheduled terrorism-volume references, and telegram boundaries without treating requests as completed arrests/extraditions/prosecutions or recasting the record as generic intelligence/public law.
- An economic/foreign-assistance budget PDF must preserve fiscal-year cycle, requested amounts, program accounts, IFI/debt terms, OMB/Treasury/AID/IDCA/State/Defense lanes, attached budget annexes, source-family evidence, and document boundaries without treating requests as appropriations, obligations, disbursements, IFI decisions, or country allocations.
- A human-rights/refugee/global-issues PDF must preserve issue lane, legal/program authority, public/congressional/scientific/international-organization context, population or country scope, public-health or relief metrics, attached policy-paper status, follow-on signature or determination evidence, and document boundaries without treating drafts or policy discussions as final signed determinations, funding actions, certifications, treaty ratifications, implementation, or delivered relief.
- A military-crisis/operational PDF must preserve crisis or operation name, theater, units/assets, exercise or port-call status, deployment/evacuation/strike options, ROE or authorization evidence, host-nation support, military assistance/cooperation, DOD/JCS/State/NSC lanes, scheduled-publication cross-references, and boundaries without treating proposals, options, or support offers as executed deployments, exercises, approvals, or delivered assistance.
- A foreign-government or international-organization PDF must preserve issuing body, foreign delegation/government position, UN/OAS/NATO/European Community/IFI/secretariat lane, document symbol or resolution/communique status, adoption/circulation/language/version status, U.S. archival-copy lane, distribution, negative-search claims, and boundaries without recasting the item as an ordinary telegram, generic treaty item, public source, or translation.
- A visual-material/source-image PDF must preserve map/photograph/chart/diagram identity, caption/title, source-image or appendix target, relationship to the selected document, printed/not-found/attached-but-not-printed status, and document boundaries without inventing image content, captions, links, appendix relationships, or printed status from a textual reference.
- An image-only facsimile or handwritten appendix page must request transcription/source-image authority when visual evidence is insufficient.
- An editor-transcribed handwritten-source PDF must preserve transcription status, source-image or appendix relationship, uncertain/illegible readings, strikeouts, underlining, bullets, dashes, equals signs, indentation, list hierarchy, meeting/support evidence, and boundaries without recasting the selected notes as a polished memorandum, formal meeting record, or image-only appendix.
- A release/declassification/source-image packet must keep RAC/NLR/FOIA/MDR identifiers, source-control numbers, declassification stamps, source-image locators, government-copy variants, sanitization/withdrawal status, not-declassified placeholders, scan quality, and selected-document boundaries separate from archival source-provenance fields.
- A withheld-in-full selected document must preserve document number, heading, source note, classification/handling, page count not declassified, and absent-body status without inventing text, treating it as OCR failure, or demoting it to source-register triage.
- An excerpted or omitted-body selected document must preserve the printed excerpt scope, source note, omission statement, classification/handling, portion markings, and adjacent boundaries without inventing the omitted text, treating the PDF as truncated, or recasting the case as withheld-in-full.
- A directive package must preserve directive number, cover memorandum, distribution, annex/tab classification, paragraph markings, and printed-elsewhere relationships as separate evidence claims.
- A policy-review/study-directive PDF must preserve review/tasking status, addressee or tasking body, review scope, deadlines, printed tabs/work plans, prior directive references, source path, classification, and boundaries without treating requested recommendations, options, gap lists, or draft instructions as approved decisions or transmitted instructions.
- A National Security Study Directive PDF must preserve terms of reference, required report/output, responsible interdepartmental group, follow-on NSDD/NSD relationship, attached timetable status, handling limits, and cover-memo/boundary evidence without treating the study tasking as the final policy decision or negotiating instruction.
- An organization-management PDF must preserve office/role, staff-structure, committee/participant-list scope, effective-date, recordkeeping, organizational-tab status, related-volume target, and document-boundary evidence without turning proposed structures into implemented reforms, meeting-specific attendance into standing membership, acting service into confirmed office, or missing tabs into printed organizational content.
- A transition or reader-marked PDF must preserve source family, pre-inaugural/transition status, reader/recipient action, initials/signature basis, marginalia text and location, routing/read-by status, copy variant, attachment status, and document boundaries without converting reader markings into body text, routing into approval, or a specialized source family into a generic archive label.
- A negotiating-instructions PDF must preserve wrapper directive or cover-memo evidence, negotiation forum, round/session, delegation addressee, overall and group-specific instructions, reftels/septels, draft-versus-sent telegram status, tabled proposal text, attached-but-not-printed guidance, and adjacent telegram boundaries without treating authorized proposals as proof of actions taken.
- A table/chart-heavy PDF must preserve rows, columns, labels, units, declassification placeholders, and page-boundary spillover; it must not silently merge the start of the next numbered document into the selected document.
- A memcon/telcon PDF must preserve meeting/call metadata, participant-list layout, side labels, notetakers/interpreters, excerpt boundaries, full-record relationships, and negative-search claims separately.
- A shorthand meeting-notes PDF must preserve selected-note status, note title, speaker initials, terse fragments, original handwritten-note or transcription basis, Daily Diary timing support, and `No minutes were found` separately without expanding initials, polishing fragments, inferring attendance from diary support, or recasting the record as formal minutes/full memcon.
- A night-note or evening-report PDF must preserve selected-update status, intended recipient, title/status line, undated heading, source-note drafting/clearance or approval date, and read-status caveats such as `There is no indication Reagan saw this report` without treating address to the President as proof of reading, approval, or action.
- A Daily Diary or schedule PDF must support chronology and meeting metadata without becoming substantive minutes, a participant list, or proof of conversation content unless the evidence explicitly says so.
- A retrospective or personal-account PDF must preserve author, account type, entry/recollection date, publication date, page or folio locator, quote boundaries, official-record gaps, contemporaneous official evidence, and public/source citation separately without converting memoirs, oral histories, or personal diaries into formal minutes, official transcripts, or decision records.
- A telegram/cable PDF must preserve header metadata, CFPF/Electronic Telegrams identifiers, transmission/event/date distinctions, reference telegrams, and page-boundary spillover separately.
- A treaty-text or international-agreement PDF must preserve parties, title, signature place/date, article/paragraph hierarchy, definitions, numerical limits, verification/inspection provisions, integral annex/protocol/MOU/agreed-statement status, authentic-language statement, signatory blocks, source/publication basis, and document boundaries without recasting the selected instrument as a transmittal, Senate message, public remarks, policy paper, telegram, or generic public-source item.
- A treaty/transmittal PDF must not collapse transmittal letter, treaty text, protocols, annexes, MOUs, analyses, presidential message, Senate ratification, and entry-into-force evidence into one generic attachment.
- A translated or foreign-language PDF must not collapse original-language status, translation date, translator/office evidence, original-bracket notes, omitted material, annex status, and next-document spillover into a generic English-language source note.
- An intelligence or estimative PDF must not collapse agency product number, source family, information-as-of date, receipt stamp, paragraph handling restrictions, and not-declassified placeholders into one generic classification label.
- An editorial-note PDF must not be forced into archival source-note form and must not import a following document's source note when the excerpt includes the next numbered item.
- A correspondence PDF must unitize paired letters separately and must not collapse letter date, delivery/backchannel timestamp, copy status, translation status, response cross-reference, and adjacent document boundaries into one generic source note.
- An action or information memorandum PDF must preserve memo wrapper metadata, action-versus-information status, subject line, recipient channel, drafter/clearance, sensitivity controls, reader initials or stamped review evidence, recommendation or decision-request language, marginalia, and adjacent document boundaries without treating recommendations, proposed priorities, approval/disapproval lines, or reader comments as completed decisions unless visible evidence supports that status.
- A briefing or talking-points PDF must preserve memo wrapper metadata, recommendation language, nested bullet hierarchy, stamped review evidence, cross-references, and adjacent document boundaries without presenting draft points as actions actually taken.
- A briefing-book or read-ahead packet PDF must preserve cover/title list, paper inventory, printed papers, unprinted listed-paper status, target recipient/event, packet date, individual paper date or undated status, paper-specific drafting/clearance, source path, and prior/following document boundaries without flattening the packet into one ordinary memo or promoting listed-but-unprinted papers into selected text.
- A PROFS/electronic-message PDF must preserve sender, recipient, subject, date/time, thread/printout/source-system metadata, copied recipients, and boundary evidence separately and must not be mistaken for an ordinary telegram/cable unless visible evidence shows that form.
- A printed-attachment PDF must distinguish the wrapper document from the printed attachment, keep attachment-specific classification and marginalia separate, preserve attached-but-not-printed materials as attachment-publication-status evidence, and avoid importing a following document's attachment cross-reference into the selected document.
- An interview/transcript PDF must preserve speaker-turn labels, interviewer/interviewee/outlet metadata, transcript source, condensed or published version, omissions, ending time, and adjacent document boundaries without recasting the item as a memcon, telcon, meeting minutes, or generic public-source excerpt.
- A recorded proceeding or tape-transcript PDF must preserve event/forum identity, transcript-source basis, title-page/program omission, moderator/chair, named and generic speaker labels, off-record/confidentiality context, inaudible/uncertain transcription markers, stage cues, transcript copy/cover-letter evidence, edited/public-version status, and document boundaries without recasting the item as ordinary formal minutes, a memcon/telcon, press interview, public speech, or proof of formal policy decisions.
- A formal meeting-minutes PDF must preserve the institutional meeting body, subject or agenda block, role-based participant roster, speaker turns, decision or consultation language, Record of Decision references, source-note meeting location, and adjacent document boundaries without recasting it as a memcon, briefing paper, directive package, or generic meeting summary.
- A memorandum-for-the-record or record-note PDF must preserve record type, subject/title, meeting/session date range, drafter/preparer, attendee list, side labels, attachment status, negative no-memcon-search claims, and adjacent document boundaries without recasting the selected item as a memcon/telcon, formal minutes, telegram, decision record, or briefing memo.
- A decision-record PDF must preserve issue headings, decision formulas, agreed actions, no-decision/no-consensus language, responsible agencies, deadlines, attached-but-not-printed tab references, and adjacent record boundaries without recasting the item as formal meeting minutes, a directive, or a briefing/action memo.
- A covert-action authorization PDF must preserve proposed versus approved authority, Finding/MON/Scope Paper status, congressional notification/reporting language, attached-but-not-printed authorization instruments, no-final-signed-copy claims, operational not-declassified text, funding evidence, and packet boundaries without treating draft authority as final or collapsing it into generic intelligence analysis.
